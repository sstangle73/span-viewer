Cluster co-occurrence — which loads run together?

For every pair of clusters on different circuits, computes how often they're both active in the same minute. Lift > 1.5 means they co-occur more than chance — useful for confirming Phase 1 cluster hypotheses (e.g. an "850 W" cluster on outlet A co-occurring strongly with a "200 W" cluster on outlet B → both probably belong to the same air conditioner).

Support_A = fraction of minutes A is active. Both = minutes both are active simultaneously. Lift = observed / (support_A × support_B). 1.0 means independent; higher means correlated.
