FHMM disaggregation — split a mixed circuit into appliances

Factorial Hidden Markov Model: each of the top 6 amplitude clusters discovered by Phase 1 becomes a candidate appliance (2-state: off / on). Viterbi inference finds the most likely on/off path for each appliance given the observed total power signal. Best targets: mixed circuits like Kitchen Counter Outlets & Microwave or the Sub panel - Under sink & upstairs & attic & smokes.

Caveats: the model is unsupervised — appliance labels are heuristic, not ground truth. Similar-amplitude appliances may be confused. Increase noise σ if the model is over-fitting micro-jitter; decrease if it's missing genuine state changes.
