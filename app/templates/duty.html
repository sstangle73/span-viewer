kWh per day

From SPAN's own readings, in 10-minute buckets (SPAN keeps them for about a month). For a circuit with standby_w and on_w set, each bucket's on-time is its energy above standby over the running draw, so a 3-minute firing counts 3 minutes; otherwise a bucket averaging on_kw or more counts as 10 minutes on. A start is a running bucket after an idle one, so two cycles in neighbouring buckets count once. Older days have a daily total only: their minutes come from standby_w and on_w when set, and are blank otherwise.
