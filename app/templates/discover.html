Load discovery — what's plugged into each circuit?

Reads 3-second per-circuit power from space_live and looks for repeating on/off "events" — moments when a load above {{ min_step_w }} W kicked on, stayed on for at least 15 s, then turned off. Similar-amplitude events are clustered to surface distinct loads hiding on shared circuits.

Best targets: window ACs, microwaves, kettles, dishwasher elements, sump pumps — anything with a distinctive draw on a mixed outlet circuit. Less useful for: fixed appliances on dedicated circuits (already detectable from the 10-min data on /appliances).
